# Half-Cell Potential Mapping (ASTM C876 / DIN EN ISO 12696)

Non-destructive technique to estimate the probability of active rebar corrosion by measuring the electrochemical potential of the steel against a reference electrode placed on the concrete surface.

## Equipment

- Reference electrode: Cu/CuSO4 (CSE) most common; alternatives Ag/AgCl, Mn/MnO2.
- High-impedance voltmeter (>10 MΩ).
- Connection to rebar (drilled contact point).
- Wet sponge / wetting agent for surface contact.

## Procedure (short)

1. Establish electrical continuity of the rebar mesh.
2. Pre-wet the concrete surface to lower contact resistance.
3. Measure on a grid (typ. 0.5 × 0.5 m, denser in suspect zones).
4. Record potential, location, temperature, moisture state.
5. Plot equipotential contour map.

## Interpretation (ASTM C876, CSE)

| Potential E [mV vs. CSE] | Corrosion probability |
|--------------------------|-----------------------|
| E > −200                  | < 10 % (passive)      |
| −350 ≤ E ≤ −200          | uncertain             |
| E < −350                  | > 90 % (active)       |

Note: Absolute thresholds shift with moisture, cover, chloride, carbonation. Potential **gradients** (>100 mV/m) are often more diagnostic than absolute values.

## Combine with

- Chloride profile (acid-soluble, % by cement mass).
- Carbonation depth (phenolphthalein test).
- Cover meter survey.
- Resistivity (Wenner 4-probe).

Only the combination of these methods supports a defensible rehabilitation strategy under *Instandsetzungs-Richtlinie* / ZTV-ING.
