# kks-corrosion-notes

Study notes on **cathodic corrosion protection** (German: *Kathodischer Korrosionsschutz* — KKS) for reinforced concrete and steel structures, plus concrete rehabilitation. Written in English with German technical terms in parentheses, to support my coursework at **Technische Hochschule Deggendorf** (Master, Civil & Environmental Engineering).

## Why this repo

KKS is one of the few corrosion protection methods that *stops* ongoing corrosion of rebar in chloride-contaminated concrete, rather than only delaying it. The topic mixes electrochemistry, structural condition assessment, and German standards (DIN EN ISO 12696). These notes are my study companion.

## Key topics covered

- Steel passivation and depassivation through carbonation / chloride ingress
- Half-cell potential measurement (Cu/CuSO₄ reference electrode)
- Impressed Current Cathodic Protection (ICCP) vs. galvanic / sacrificial anodes
- Anode materials: MMO titanium mesh, conductive coatings, zinc layer anodes
- Acceptance criteria per DIN EN ISO 12696 (100 mV decay, instant-off potential)
- Interaction with concrete repair principles per Instandsetzungs-Richtlinie
